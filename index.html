Twice the Magic.

We’re Jeanie and Patti — identical twins, BSN nurses, and the sisters behind Three Wishes. We spend our days caring for patients and our nights preheating ovens.

Three Wishes began with founder Jeanie’s name and a promise we still keep: every box should feel like a wish granted.

Jeanie, founder, runs the cookies and bars — the close-your-eyes-on-the-first-bite kind. Patti, co-founder, is the bread half: one long-fermented sourdough bake a month, made the way bread used to be made.

For years we’ve been the ones baking for our own units and workplaces — hospital teams, office celebrations, organizations like Planned Parenthood. Now the same recipes ship to your door.

Jeanie's cookie care

Made with care. Kept with care.

Simple storage and warming advice, so every batch tastes the way it did leaving our oven.

Cookie care

Warm like it's fresh.

Airtight container up to 5 days at room temp, or freeze up to 3 months. Bring back that just-baked hug: 350°F oven for 5–7 min, or microwave 10–15 sec (+20 sec if frozen).

Take-&-bake dough

Keep cold. Bake hot.

Cookie Dough Sleeves live in the freezer. Preheat to 350°F, arrange pucks on a lined sheet, and bake straight from frozen for warm-from-Jeanie's-kitchen magic.

Shipping & allergens

Shipped with care.

Orders ship Mondays & Wednesdays in temperature-controlled packaging, 1–2 business day delivery. Contains wheat, milk, eggs, and soy. May contain traces of nuts.

Allergy information — please read

Three Wishes Bakery is not a nut-free, peanut-free, gluten-free, dairy-free, or allergen-free kitchen. Our treats contain or may come into contact with wheat, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts, and tree nuts. Flavors rotate weekly, so please review each product's details before ordering. If you have a severe allergy, our kitchen may not be the safest option for you — and we'd rather tell you that honestly.

Good to know

Cookie questions, answered.

Do you deliver cookies nationwide?

Yes — every order is baked to order and shipped anywhere in the USA in temperature-controlled packaging, arriving in 1–2 business days. Free shipping on orders $100+.

Do you offer local pickup or delivery?

Yes — pickup in Riverside County is free. Local delivery is available for a distance-based fee (it's a big county!); we confirm the fee with you before anything is charged.

What is the Mystery Loaf?

Patti bakes one secret sourdough flavor each month. You find out what it is when it arrives — that's the fun.

How do the weekly drops work?

The menu reveals Sundays on Instagram, orders open Mondays, and limited boxes ship Monday & Wednesday. The Classic Wish is always on the menu.

Are you really nurses?

Yes — twin BSN nurses, working shifts and working ovens. Cookie care is literally in our job description.

How do I win a free treat?

Make your three wishes below — every month founder Jeanie grants one, adds it to the menu, and sends the winner theirs free.
