Refunds

Refunds & returns.

Last updated 6 August 2026

⚠ Confirm before launch

The highlighted items below need Jeanie and Patti to confirm the real answer. Placeholder text in a policy is worse than no policy — a customer can hold you to whatever it says.

Everything is baked fresh to order, so this is not a "change your mind" return policy — it's a "we got it wrong, we'll fix it" policy.

If something arrives wrong

Email hello@3wishesbakery.com within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo. If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or in poor condition, we will replace it on the next bake or refund it in full — your choice.

If it never arrives

If tracking shows delivered but you don't have it, check with your household and neighbours first, then tell us. If a carrier loses a box, we replace or refund it — that isn't your problem to absorb.

If it's delayed in transit

We ship perishable food in insulated packaging for 1–2 day delivery. If a carrier delay means your box arrives in unacceptable condition, that counts as arriving wrong — see above.

Cancelling and changing an order

Confirm the real cutoff. Because we buy ingredients and bake to order, changes and cancellations are only possible before we start baking your batch. As a starting point we suggest: cancel or change free of charge up to 24 hours before your ship date; after that we can't, because it's already made.

What we can't refund

  • Orders where the shipping address given to us was wrong
  • Food that has been eaten, other than a genuine quality complaint
  • Take-and-bake dough that wasn't kept frozen
  • Dislike of a flavour — including Patti's Mystery Loaf, which is sold as a surprise. If you have allergies or strong dislikes, order a known flavour instead.

Catering and large orders

Confirm: deposit amount, whether it's refundable, and the cancellation window for events. Event orders are usually agreed individually and we will put the terms in writing before taking a deposit.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, via Stripe, and usually appear within 5–10 business days.
